❓ FAQs

How do I book a ride? +
Go to "Book a Ride", enter your pickup and drop locations, choose a vehicle, select date & time, and click Confirm. You must be logged in to book.
How is the fare calculated? +
Fare = Base Fare + (Distance × ₹8/km) + 5% GST. It is an estimate based on approximate distance and may vary slightly.
Can I cancel a booking? +
Yes! Go to "My Bookings" and click Cancel next to any active booking. Cancellations are completely free of charge.
What payment methods are accepted? +
You can pay cash or via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) directly to the driver on arrival. We do not store any payment information.
